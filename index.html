Clarity

No installs. No accounts. No files left behind.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Every tool here runs in the browser. Open the link, use the tool, close the tab — nothing is installed and nothing is left behind on your machine.

Do I have to create an account?

No accounts anywhere. There is nothing to sign up for, nothing to verify, and no profile behind the hub. If a tool needed one, it would not be listed here.

What happens to the clips I enhance?

They are processed and handed back as a download. TOOLAPIS itself stores nothing: the hub is a set of links, not a service with a database.

Does USB tethering need a second device?

Yes — an Android phone and a USB cable. Gnirehtet shares your PC's connection over the cable itself, so no extra modem, router or SIM is involved.
